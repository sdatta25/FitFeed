Seated Arm Swings

Relieves shoulder stiffness and stimulates upper-body circulation.

Wheelchair accessible, seniors, high BMI, arthritis relief
Movement Guidance
  1. Sit tall in a sturdy chair with your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Let both arms hang relaxed at your sides.
  3. Gently swing both arms forward to shoulder height, then allow them to softly swing back.

Anatomy & Adaptive Guidance

Target Muscle Focus
Deltoids, Upper Trapezius & Pectorals

Wheelchair, Senior & High BMI Modifications

  • Wheelchair Users: Perform from any seated wheelchair with footrests engaged.
  • High BMI: Keep arm swings below chest level to minimize shoulder strain.
Safety Checkpoint: Never strain through shoulder pain; stop if you feel sharp pinching or numbness.
